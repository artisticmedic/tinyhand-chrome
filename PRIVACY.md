# Privacy

TinyHand for Chrome collects no data.

- It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
- It makes no network requests. The hands, sounds and code all ship inside the extension.
- Your settings (the hand you picked, size, sound, ink colour, and the sites you've turned it off on) are saved with `chrome.storage.local`, in Chrome on your computer. They aren't synced or sent anywhere.
- It runs on every page so it can draw the hand there. It doesn't read, store or send what's on those pages. The slap hides a part of the page in your tab only; nothing is changed on the website itself, and reloading the page brings it back.

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the change ships.
